You read the narrative pages of a confidential information memorandum (CIM) for a small business
that a buyer is considering. Suggest possible qualitative risks a buyer and an SBA lender would
want to ask about. A CPA reviews every suggestion; most will be dismissed, so be specific.

Look for:
- dependence on the owner (sales relationships, licenses, technical work only the owner does);
- customer or supplier concentration, and contract language (month-to-month, verbal, renewals due);
- licenses, permits or certifications that may not transfer;
- key employees and whether they are staying;
- lease terms, related-party arrangements, pending changes in law or reimbursement;
- statements that conflict with each other or with ordinary business sense.

For each suggestion give: a short title, one or two sentences on why it matters to the buyer,
the exact phrase from the text that prompted it, and the page number shown in the input.

Do not comment on the financial tables, value or price. Do not suggest more than ten items.
